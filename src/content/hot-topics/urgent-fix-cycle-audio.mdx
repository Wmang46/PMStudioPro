Is your team stuck fixing the same kinds of problems over and over? That's not a people problem—it's a process problem. In this audio, we break down the Urgent Fix Cycle: what causes it, what it costs, and how to systematically get out of it.

## What You'll Hear

- Why unclear requirements are the root of most urgent fixes
- The real business cost of late defect discovery
- What a structured, predictable delivery system actually looks like
- A three-phase improvement approach: Stabilize, Align, Improve
- How to run a pilot without overhauling the entire organization

## The Three Phases

- **Stabilize** — control the intake, define "ready for dev," limit mid-cycle changes
- **Align** — shared backlog, central work system, cross-functional refinement before build
- **Improve** — shift testing left, define done, track defect leakage

## The Key Insight

The teams that escape the urgent fix cycle don't just work harder—they fix the system. When requirements are clear and teams are aligned, stable releases stop being the exception and start being the norm.
